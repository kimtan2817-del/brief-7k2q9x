About this brief

Each card is the publisher's own opening text, trimmed to about 60 words, with a link to the original. Topic labels and the TOP OF THE DAY flag come from simple keyword rules, not from a person or an AI, so treat them as quick signals and open the story to confirm. Pictures belong to the publishers and are credited on each card. Stories older than 3 hours drop off, except the day's top stories.

Prices come from free public sources and may be delayed. FD rates are entered by hand.
